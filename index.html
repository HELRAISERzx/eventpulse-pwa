THE ARCHITECTURAL SHIFT

Why Traditional Navigation Fails Indoors

Massive convention halls create steel Faraday cages and extreme crowd densities where standard mobile apps collapse.

⚠️ THE CONVENTION PROBLEM
  • GPS Blind Spots: Satellite signals cannot penetrate multi-story concrete and steel exhibition halls, rendering blue dots useless.
  • Dangerous Choke Points: Sudden post-keynote room exits cause high-density surges and stampede hazards in main corridors.
  • Inaccessible Barriers: Unmarked stairways trap wheelchair users and attendees with mobility equipment or strollers.
  • Group Separation: Cellular network congestion severs group communication, leading to lost flock members and dead batteries.
⚡ THE EVENTPULSE SOLUTION
  • Zero-Hardware Anchors: High-visibility 2-character pillar codes (A1–D2) & instant QR calibration without BLE beacons.
  • Stochastic Rerouting: Algorithmic crowd split that probabilistically distributes foot traffic across parallel hallways.
  • A* Step-Free Solver: Certified elevator-only paths highlighted with an animated emerald ribbon and stair warnings.
  • Flock Tethering & Failover: P2P proximity radar with automatic leader torch-pass when battery drops under 10%.

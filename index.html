⚡ Architecture & Innovation Matrix

Engineered specifically to solve critical indoor positioning, crowd safety, and accessibility challenges.

LOCALIZATION

Zero-GPS Pillar Anchors

Lightweight 2-character pillar anchors (A1–D2) and instant QR scans calibrate attendee coordinates without expensive BLE beacons or unreliable indoor Wi-Fi triangulation.

ACCESSIBILITY

A* Step-Free Route Engine

Mathematical A* graph solver that dynamically excludes stairways and steep corridors, rendering certified elevator-safe paths highlighted with an emerald ribbon and stair warnings.

SAFETY

Stochastic Anti-Stampede Dispersion

Prevents dangerous secondary bottlenecks by probabilistically splitting attendee traffic across multiple parallel walkways when an emergency or room relocation occurs.

INTELLIGENCE

COLLABORATION
